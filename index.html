Crosstalk Matrix (Trigger Engine)

Row = the pad being checked, column = the other pad it's compared against. Value = how big a % of the column pad's peak this row pad needs to reach, within its Crosstalk Lock window, to count as a genuine hit instead of bleed-through. 0 = never reject (use for pads that never bleed into each other on your hardware). Hit pads one at a time and watch the Live Visualizer on the Dashboard tab to find which pairs actually bleed -- distance on the pad layout does NOT reliably predict this on a rigid/plastic base.

Roland-Style Crosstalk Cancel Level

Choose one pad and apply a 50%, 60%, 70% or 80% level to that pad's matrix row. This matches the OLED Crosstalk Level control. Other pads' rows are not changed.
